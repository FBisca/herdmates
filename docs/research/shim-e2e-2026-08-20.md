## Bugs found (filed as issues)

1. **zsh autocorrect swallows the split launch.** `--split` runs the
   claude command line through the user's interactive zsh; with
   `CORRECT` set, zsh prompts `correct 'claude' to '.claude' [nyae]?`
   and the lead pane hangs silently until a human answers `n`.
   Launcher should exec claude directly (or `setopt nocorrect` /
   non-interactive shell).
2. **Plugin-pane board dies silently with >1 team dir.** The
   `open-pane-board` action cannot pass `--team`; with multiple
   `~/.claude/teams/` entries (stale dirs from ended sessions count),
   pane-board prints the refusal and exits — the pane closes before a
   human can read it. Needs an in-TUI team picker or stale-team
   filtering instead of exit.
